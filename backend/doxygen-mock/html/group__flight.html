Flight Control

Inner-loop flight-control laws for the Super Hornet demonstration model. Commands are produced at 80 Hz and consumed by the actuator interface.

Responsibilities

  • Pitch, roll, and yaw rate damping.
  • Angle-of-attack and load-factor limiting.
  • Control-surface mixing for trailing-edge flaps and tailerons.

Public types

FlightControl is the primary façade. The generated header is fcs.h.

Example

auto cmd = fcs.step(sample);
actuators.write(cmd.taileron_l, cmd.taileron_r, cmd.rudder);

Limits

Load factor is clamped to the structural envelope published in the air-data table. Values outside that envelope are rejected and latched for maintenance.
